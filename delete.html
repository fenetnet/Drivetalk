Delete your DriveBond account

  • In the app (immediate): Settings → "Delete my account and my data" → confirm.
  • Without the app: email fenet.net@gmail.com with your name in the app (and phone number, if you added one). Deleted within 7 days.
  • What is deleted: everything — name, phone number, friends, circles, ratings, availability, offers, profile photo, device identifiers, reports and usage measurements. Feedback text you sent via "Send feedback" is kept without any link to you (no name, no account).
